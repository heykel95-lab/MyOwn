\chapter{Recorded Experimental Data}
\label{app:recorded_data}
\ReviewMark{green}{The appendix records the signals actually used in the
evaluation and identifies the common configured surface reference used by the controller
and analysis.}

During torque control, the controller stores the experimental signals in a
preallocated ring buffer inside the
\(1\,\mathrm{kHz}\) callback. After the controller stops, the retained rows
are written to a CSV file in chronological order.

\section{Evaluation Signals}
\ReviewMark{green}{The table is a useful reproducibility record and keeps units,
frames, and model-estimated quantities explicit.}

\Added{\mbox{Table~\ref{tab:csv_column_groups}} lists the recorded signal
groups used to calculate the reported surface-contact and null-space results.
The surface-contact schema supplies the contact-geometry and commanded-wrench
groups, and the Cartesian pose-hold schema supplies the singular-value,
projected-motion, and disturbance groups. The complete file schemas, including
the diagnostic fields that no reported result uses, are documented with the
recorded data.}

\Added{A suffix \texttt{\_x}, \texttt{\_y}, or \texttt{\_z} denotes a
base-frame Cartesian component, and a suffix \texttt{\_1} to \texttt{\_7}
denotes a joint component.}
