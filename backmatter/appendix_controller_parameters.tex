\subsection{Calibration Relations}

For the seated surface pose, the nominal end-effector axis and the tool-face
centre offset supplied the stored plane quantities,

\begin{equation}
n_s=R_{\mathrm{EE}}[0,0,1]^\top,
\qquad
p_s=p_{\mathrm{EE}}+R_{\mathrm{EE}}r_{\mathrm{face,EE}}.
\label{eq:appendix_surface_calibration}
\end{equation}

The first tangent was the normalised projection of the base-frame
\mbox{\(x\)} direction onto the plane, and
\mbox{\(t_2=n_s\times t_1\)} completed the surface frame. For the tool-normal
calibration, the fitted direction was

\begin{equation}
n_{\mathrm{Tool,EE}}
=
\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{\lVert n\rVert=1}
\sum_{i<j}
\left\lVert(R_{\mathrm{cal},i}-R_{\mathrm{cal},j})n\right\rVert^2.
\label{eq:appendix_tool_normal_fit}
\end{equation}

Three seated yaw captures entered the fit. The sign of the fitted eigenvector
was selected towards nominal \mbox{\(+Z_{\mathrm{EE}}\)}.
