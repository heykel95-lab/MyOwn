\subsection{Calibration Relations}

For the seated surface-reference pose, the nominal end-effector
\mbox{\(Z_{\mathrm{EE}}\)} axis and the calibrated tool-face-centre offset
were used to calculate the stored plane quantities. The tool face rests against
the plane with \mbox{\(+Z_{\mathrm{EE}}\)} directed into it, so the outward
normal is the opposite direction,

\begin{equation}
n_s=-R_{\mathrm{EE}}[0,0,1]^\top,
\qquad
p_s=p_{\mathrm{EE}}+R_{\mathrm{EE}}r_{\mathrm{face,EE}}.
\label{eq:appendix_surface_calibration}
\end{equation}

The first tangent was obtained by projecting the base-frame
\mbox{\(x_0\)} direction onto the configured plane and normalising the
result, and
\mbox{\(t_2=n_s\times t_1\)} completed the surface frame. For the tool-normal
calibration, the fitted direction was

\begin{equation}
n_{\mathrm{Tool,EE}}
=
\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{\lVert \tilde n\rVert=1}
\sum_{i<j}
\left\lVert(R_{\mathrm{cal},i}-R_{\mathrm{cal},j})\tilde n\right\rVert^2.
\label{eq:appendix_tool_normal_fit}
\end{equation}

Three seated yaw captures entered the fit. The sign of the fitted eigenvector
was selected towards nominal \mbox{\(+Z_{\mathrm{EE}}\)}.

\section{Damping and Centre of Compliance}

\mbox{\(r_c=p_c-p_{\mathrm{TCP}}\)}, expressed in the robot base frame. For
a tool-fixed centre, the configuration specifies its end-effector components
\mbox{\(r_{c,\mathrm{EE}}\)}, and the controller calculates
\mbox{\(r_c=R_{\mathrm{EE}}r_{c,\mathrm{EE}}\)}. The damping calculation
and the compliance-centre transformation are defined in
\mbox{\Cref{subsec:inertia_scaled_damping_calculation,sec:centre_of_compliance}}.
