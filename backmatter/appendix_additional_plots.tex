\mbox{\(-10\)}, \mbox{\(+10\)}, \mbox{\(+20\)}, and
\mbox{\(+40\,\mathrm{mm}\)} along the tool axis at
\mbox{\(\theta_{\mathrm{offset},t_1}=+10^\circ\)}. The corresponding
achieved pose-based initial angular offsets were approximately
\mbox{\(+9.3^\circ\)}. Each of

to \mbox{\(+7.60^\circ\)} between \mbox{\(-40\)} and
\mbox{\(+40\,\mathrm{mm}\)}. The \mbox{\(7.73^\circ\)} Case-D tangential span
was about 23 times larger than this \mbox{\(0.34^\circ\)} tool-axis span in
\mbox{\Cref{fig:supporting_tool_axis}}. The tangential projection
\mbox{\(\lVert r_{c,t}\rVert=\lVert r_c\rVert\sin\phi_0\)} supplies the
normal-press moment, by
\mbox{\Cref{eq:coc_normal_press_tangential_only}}. At
\mbox{\(\phi_0=9.30^\circ\)} and
\mbox{\(\lVert r_c\rVert=40\,\mathrm{mm}\)}, this projection is
\mbox{\(6.47\,\mathrm{mm}\)}. Its small magnitude is consistent with the
measured response span.}
