\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\ReviewMark{green}{The abstract states the problem, implemented mechanism,
principal measured ordering, null-space result, and scope limits without
overloading the reader with the full parameter matrix.}

Angular tool--surface mismatch causes edge-first contact. Under rigid position
control, the robot resists the aligning rotation and develops contact forces
and moments.

A real-time, phase-dependent Cartesian impedance controller was implemented
for a seven-degree-of-freedom (\abbr{DOF}) Franka Emika Panda. During contact
set-up, stiffness and damping defined about a selected virtual centre of
compliance \(p_c\) were transformed to the tool centre point. The resulting coupling
allowed the commanded pressing motion along the surface normal \(n_s\) to generate a
corrective moment.

The experiments varied directional stiffness and the direction and magnitude
of the compliance-centre lever \(r_c\). Within the tested range, the in-plane
compliance-centre position produced the largest change. The assisting lever was
perpendicular to the commanded rotation axis \(u_{\mathrm{rot}}\). Reversing the commanded tool
orientation offset also reversed the assisting lever direction. This selection
gave about \(6^\circ\) improvement in both tangent-axis tests. The best tested
lever magnitude depended on the commanded rotation axis \(u_{\mathrm{rot}}\) and offset angle.
Higher rotational stiffness reduced alignment, with axis-dependent behaviour.

\Revised{For four commanded tool orientation offsets in the surface tangent plane, the
direction-selected \mbox{\(60\,\mathrm{mm}\)} compliance-centre lever
\mbox{\(r_c\)} improved
alignment in every case. Using one fixed lever instead reduced the matched
alignment improvement \mbox{\(\Delta\theta_{\mathrm{align}}\)} by
\mbox{\(32\)--\(48\,\%\)}, supporting selection of the lever
direction from the commanded tool orientation offset.}

Complementary Cartesian pose-hold trials showed that projected null-space
damping reduced redundant motion. Both tested conditioning torque commands
returned the minimum singular value \(\sigma_{\min}\) to approximately its initial level after
the disturbance.
