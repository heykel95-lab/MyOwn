\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This thesis investigates the design, implementation, and evaluation of a real-time Cartesian impedance controller for the Franka Emika Panda, a redundant 7-\abbr{DOF} collaborative robot. The main objective is to achieve compliant behaviour in Cartesian space while maintaining a clear relation between position-and-orientation error, impedance forces and moments, Jacobian mapping, and joint torque commands. Special attention is given to stiffness and damping matrices expressed in the end-effector frame and transformed into the robot base frame.

The controller is implemented in C++ using libfranka and Eigen within a real-time torque-control callback. Logged CSV data are used to evaluate set-point convergence, trajectory tracking, compliance under external interaction, and commanded joint torques. The results show damped convergence toward the desired pose, bounded trajectory tracking errors, stiffness-dependent compliance behaviour, and smooth torque commands for the evaluated parameter sets.
